## 🐞 Reporting a Bug / 报告 Bug

Please use the [Bug Report template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md) and include:

1. **What you did** (steps to reproduce)
2. **What you expected**
3. **What actually happened** (with logs, screenshots)
4. **Environment** (OS, Python version, deployment mode)

A minimal reproducible example is the most helpful thing you can provide.
